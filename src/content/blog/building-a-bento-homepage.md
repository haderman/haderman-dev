A personal homepage does not need to explain everything at once. A bento layout makes room for a few focused surfaces: a profile, a project, a small experiment, or a note worth sharing.

The useful part is not the grid itself. It is the constraint. Each card gets a clear job, and the page remains easy to scan as new work is added.

For this site, the blog is another card in that system. The latest note gets the most space, while a few selected posts provide a quick path into older ideas.
